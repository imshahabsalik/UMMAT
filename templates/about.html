VISION

UMMAT Foundation is a platform for all manufacturers and traders, through which they can know each other and eliminate the differences between them, where they can strongly and genuinely help each other and society at large. UMMAT Foundation aims to help the needy, poor and deprived people and to make them stand strongly and independently.

‘If awakened, we are alive’

The one thing to keep in mind is that the biggest strength we have within us is to invite and welcome others to join our hands for the betterment of society; and nobody can take this from us. To help those who are in dark and bring them in light is ordered by our Lord.

Our main motto is to invite and unite the Ummat (people). We want to step into your ideology and make you attentive and alert that will lead to the construction of a better society, a better nation and a better world. UMMAT Foundation is an important parameter in changing the world.

‘United, we advance’
MISSIONS
  1. To combine all the large and small-scale manufacturers and traders from all over the world and to make them know and help each other on a common platform.
  2. To shorten the distance between manufacturers and traders and to increase trade and business.
  3. To make the manufacturers and traders aware of new technologies and trends so that they may use them.
  4. To work for the benefits and progress of the country by linking UMMAT with all the unions/federations/associations of the nation and the world.
  5. To make the fellow members aware about the schemes and policies of state and central Government.
  6. To increase the livelihood for women by establishing small scale industries or business units under cooperative society sector through UMMAT.
  7. To increase the trade and business at national and international level.
  8. To work for the Employment Exchange for the youth and provide them suitable training & internships.
  9. To establish committees at district and state level and thus bring an efficient coordination and harmony in different aspects of work.
  10. To make proper utilization of collected charity, donations and zakaat.
  11. To advertize and popularize the idea and vision of UMMAT through social media and other platforms.
  12. To prepare an offline and online digital directory of UMMAT like ‘The Yellow Pages’.
  13. To lend help to every section and aspect of society and to initiate and regularly inspect important tasks for the betterment of the nation.

GOAL

To combine Ummat (people), through UMMAT Foundation.

A cooperative society will be made under the banner of UMMAT in which Unani medicines will be made. The network for the same will be constructed by the members of UMMAT, who will be the agents, distributors and C&F. The medicines will be prepared under the guidance of good doctors. The earned dividend, thereof, will be given to the women and their families in need, to provide them financial aid and stability. The youth who have done B.Tech, MBA, polytechnic, and other technical courses and are worried for their training, will be given the same in registered companies and will be paid for the services done by them.
